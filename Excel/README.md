# Excel Portfolio

This section contains Excel practice workbooks covering data cleaning, logical formulas, lookup and dynamic-array functions, sorting, statistical functions, rounding, and aggregation.

## Workbook collection

### Data cleaning and reconciliation
- `Data-Cleaning/Cleaning-Data-Solution.xlsx` — supplier invoice statement preparation, text extraction and concatenation, date/workday calculations, reconciliation analysis, and ASCII-table exercises.

### Formula and analysis exercises
- `Exercises/Logical-Functions/Exercise-01-Solution.xlsx` — logical and conditional calculations.
- `Exercises/Lookup-Functions/Exercise-02-Solution.xlsx` — INDEX/MATCH and VLOOKUP practice.
- `Exercises/Sorting-Data/Exercise-03-Solution.xlsx` — sorting and arranging sales and athlete datasets.
- `Exercises/Dynamic-Array-Functions/Exercise-02-Answer.xlsx` — UNIQUE, FILTER, SORT, XLOOKUP, and RAND.
- `Exercises/Lookup-and-Logic/Exercise-03-Answer.xlsx` — two-way XLOOKUP, CHOOSE, and SWITCH.
- `Exercises/Statistical-Functions/Exercise-04-Answer.xlsx` — AVERAGE, MEDIAN, MODE, LARGE, SMALL, and ranking.
- `Exercises/Rounding-and-Aggregation/Exercise-05-Answer.xlsx` — ROUNDUP, MROUND, and AGGREGATE.

## Skills demonstrated

- Cleaning and transforming text fields
- Working with dates and business-day calculations
- Reconciling invoice and report information
- Conditional counting and summation
- Lookup and reference formulas
- Sorting, filtering, and unique-value extraction
- Descriptive statistics and ranking
- Rounding and aggregation

## Notes and limitations

- These are learning and exercise workbooks, not evidence of paid client work or employment.
- Formula availability can depend on the Excel version. XLOOKUP, FILTER, and UNIQUE require a compatible modern version of Excel.
- Some workbooks contain dynamic-array formulas or formulas that may not calculate in third-party spreadsheet viewers.
- Formula presence was inspected, but every calculated result has not been independently audited. Review results in Excel before relying on them.
- Keep source files unchanged; make a separate copy before experimenting with formulas.
